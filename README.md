# Amazon Product & Marketplace Analysis


## 📌 Project Overview


**Amazon Product & Marketplace Analysis** is an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project focused on understanding product pricing, discounts, customer ratings, reviews, and estimated monthly purchases across Amazon product listings.

The objective of this project is to clean and validate the dataset, identify patterns in product performance, analyze pricing strategies, and explore relationships between customer feedback and estimated product demand using Python.


## 🎯 Project Objectives


- Clean the dataset and handle missing or inconsistent values.
- Validate listed prices and current/discounted prices.
- Analyze product pricing and discount patterns.
- Explore customer ratings and review counts.
- Use `bought_in_last_month` as an approximate measure of monthly product demand.
- Identify price, rating, review, and demand patterns.
- Detect potential outliers using statistical methods and visualizations.


## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies


- **Python** — Data analysis and preprocessing
- **Pandas** — Data cleaning and manipulation
- **NumPy** — Numerical operations
- **Matplotlib** — Data visualization
- **Seaborn** — Statistical visualization

- **Jupyter Notebook** — Interactive analysis

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis


### 1. Data Cleaning and Validation
- Checked missing values and data types.
- Converted price columns into numeric format.
- Validated listed and discounted prices.
- Handled cases such as `No Discount`.
- Examined inconsistent records and potential outliers.

### 2. Product Pricing and Discounts
- Compared listed prices with current prices.
- Analyzed discount percentages across products.
- Identified pricing patterns and potential extreme values.

### 3. Customer Ratings and Reviews
- Examined the distribution of product ratings.
- Compared review counts across products.
- Explored whether highly rated products also tend to receive more reviews.

### 4. Estimated Monthly Purchases
- Analyzed `bought_in_last_month` as an approximate demand indicator.
- Explored products with higher estimated monthly purchases.
- Investigated relationships between estimated demand, pricing, discounts, ratings, and reviews.


## 💡 Key Insights to Investigate


- **Pricing strategy:** How current prices differ from listed prices and how discounts vary across products.
- **Customer satisfaction:** Whether ratings are concentrated around particular values and how ratings vary across products.
- **Customer engagement:** Whether products with more reviews tend to have higher ratings or estimated demand.
- **Product demand:** Which products record higher estimated monthly purchases.
- **Discount effectiveness:** Whether products with larger discounts also show higher estimated monthly purchases.
- **Outlier analysis:** Whether extreme prices, review counts, ratings, or purchase estimates represent genuine high-performing products or data-quality issues.

*Note: These are the key analytical areas of the project, not confirmed statistical findings. Actual conclusions should be based on the results and visualizations in the notebook.*
